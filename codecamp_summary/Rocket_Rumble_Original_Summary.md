## Week 1 Lesson Plan Breakdown

1. **Level 1 Setup:** Select background, select and add the tiles.
2. **Build Collisions:** 
   * *What if we want to pass through some blocks but not others?*
   * **Question:** How does collision work?
   * **Answer:** Simply, if a tile has a collision active, you are not allowed to move through it. So if we want to move through a tile we need to remove the collision. Collisions are activated by default.
3. **Animations:** Add actors and assign animation to player.
4. **Actors Setup:** Add additional actors (coins, items).
5. **Placement:** Place actors in the level grid.
6. **Tile Collision Logic:** Coding: implement/code the `hitTile` function of the player.
7. **Collection Logic:** Collecting: implement/code the `collect` function of the player.
8. **Level Progression:** Next level: implement the `nextLevel` function of the player.
9. **Build More (Level 2 Scene):** Build the next level scene: *"Decorate your Level! Remember to place your Player into the level along with a ton of coins for you to collect! Unlike the first level, this level won't add coins for us. That's now OUR job! Make your level awesome! Once you're done with your second level, let your teacher know! The games with the BEST levels will be picked to go onto the Play tab on Code Camp World for everybody in the world to play!"*
10. **Customization:** Customize player movement mechanics.
