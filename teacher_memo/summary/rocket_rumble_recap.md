# Rocket Rumble — Lesson Intro & Recap Pitch

##  Welcome Back! (Lesson Recap Pitch)

> **Teacher Instructions:** Read or adapt this quick, energetic pitch to open your class. It recaps what students built previously and sets the stage for today's coding goals!

---

### "Hey everyone! Welcome back to Code Camp! 👋"

Before we dive into today’s coding mission, let's take a quick look back at what we’ve already built in **Rocket Rumble**! 🚀

*  **Setting the Stage:** We built our world! We picked an awesome background, added tile blocks, and learned the secret rule of game physics: **Collisions!** We made sure solid tiles stop our player, while leaving open paths to pass right through.
*  **Bringing it to Life:** We brought in our hero player, added sprite sheets, and hooked up cool animations!
*  **Coins & Custom Logic:** We placed coins and items across the grid, and coded up our player's core functions:
* `hitTile` — to handle running into walls and floors.
* `collect` — so our player can grab shiny loot and increase the score!
* `nextLevel` — so we can zoom off to the next stage once the coins are collected!
